# Declaration order (Go)

Within a file, after the imports:

1. `const` and `var` blocks, at the top, before any type or function.
2. Exported types and functions.
3. Unexported types and functions, after every exported one.

A method stays next to its type. An unexported helper goes below the exported functions that call it, never between them.

Doesn't apply to generated `zz_generated*` files — they're regenerated by skaff/controller-gen and edited only through their generators.
